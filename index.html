01 — THE MEANING

A festival that begins
with gratitude.

छठ पूजा सूर्य देव और छठी मैया को समर्पित एक पावन पर्व है।

Chhath Puja is one of the most distinctive festivals of the Indian subcontinent, celebrated with deep devotion especially in Bihar, Jharkhand, eastern Uttar Pradesh and Nepal's Terai region.

Devotees offer prayers to the setting and rising Sun. The observance is known for its discipline, simplicity, purity, community spirit and profound connection with water, earth, air and sunlight.

02 — THE FOUR SACRED DAYS

Four days. One journey
of devotion.

Each day has its own rhythm, preparation and meaning. Together, they form the beautiful sequence of Chhath Parv.

01
✦
DAY ONE • PURIFICATION

Nahay-Khay नहाय-खाय

The observance begins with cleanliness and a sattvic meal. Devotees bathe, clean their homes and prepare food with care, traditionally using a clean cooking space and simple ingredients.

THE FEELING A fresh beginning, purity and preparation.
02
◒✦
DAY TWO • THE SACRED MEAL

Kharna खरना

Devotees observe a fast through the day. After sunset, they offer and share prasad, commonly including jaggery kheer and roti. The rigorous निर्जला व्रत begins after Kharna prasad.

THE FEELING Restraint, devotion and sharing.
03
✺
DAY THREE • EVENING OFFERING

Sandhya Arghya संध्या अर्घ्य

Families gather at a riverbank, pond or other water body. Devotees stand in the water and offer arghya to the setting Sun, with soop and baskets filled with seasonal fruits and traditional offerings.

THE FEELING Collective prayer as daylight gently fades.
04
✿
DAY FOUR • MORNING OFFERING

Usha Arghya उषा अर्घ्य

Before sunrise, devotees return to the ghat to offer arghya to the rising Sun. Prayers are completed and the fast is broken with पारण, bringing the four-day observance to a close.

THE FEELING Hope, renewal and blessings for the days ahead.

04 — PREPARATION

Preparing the
offerings.

Every family may have its own customs and local variations. These are commonly seen items; follow your family's tradition and local guidance.

For the offerings

  • Soop and bamboo daura
  • Thekua and seasonal fruits
  • Sugarcane and coconut
  • Bananas, lemon and ginger
  • Rice, jaggery and wheat flour

For the puja

  • Diya and cotton wicks
  • Clean water and kalash
  • Flowers and leaves
  • Cloth for the offering basket
  • Traditional family-specific items

This is a general reference list, not a fixed or exhaustive ritual prescription.

07 — A FESTIVAL FOR EVERYONE

Faith, care and
shared responsibility.

Chhath brings people together. Help keep ghats clean, avoid plastic and litter, respect local customs, and make space for elders, children and people who need assistance. Follow local safety instructions near water and during crowded gatherings.

08 — GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

A few helpful basics for anyone learning about the festival for the first time.

Why is the setting Sun worshipped before the rising Sun?

Chhath uniquely honours both the setting and rising Sun. The evening offering acknowledges the Sun as it sets, while the morning offering welcomes its return and the promise of a new day.

Who observes the Chhath fast?

Traditionally, many women observe the fast, but men also participate and observe Chhath. Practices differ by family, region and personal tradition.

Why is Chhath considered a nature-centred festival?

The rituals take place near water and centre on the Sun, while offerings often include seasonal and locally grown produce. The festival expresses gratitude to the natural elements that sustain life.

Can the rituals differ between families?

Yes. Regional customs, family traditions, the items offered and the way rituals are performed can vary. Local elders and family practice are often the best guide.

What does 'Arghya' mean?

Arghya is a respectful offering of water, usually made while praying to the Sun during the evening and morning observances.
